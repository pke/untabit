Untabit couldn't register its keyboard shortcut

The suggested shortcut (Alt+U, or Ctrl+U on macOS) is most likely already taken by another extension. You can assign a different one in a few clicks — Firefox doesn't let extensions open this settings page for you:

  1. Open about:addons (Menu → Add-ons and themes)
  2. Click the gear icon ⚙ at the top right of the Extensions page
  3. Choose Manage Extension Shortcuts
  4. Find Untabit and press your preferred key combination

Until then, clicking the Untabit toolbar icon ! does the same thing as the shortcut: it pops the current tab out into its own window, and merges it back when pressed again. The red ! badge disappears once you've seen this page. If you don't see the icon, it may be hidden behind the puzzle-piece extensions menu — pin it from there.
