Untabit

Pop any tab into its own window — and back.

Alt + U

macOS: Ctrl + U

One shortcut to pop the current tab out into its own window — press it again to merge the tab back where it came from, at its old position.

The tab is moved, not reloaded: video keeps playing, scroll position and form state survive. No data access, no tracking — see the privacy policy.

Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Firefox. Store links coming soon — until then, grab it from GitHub.
